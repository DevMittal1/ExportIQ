# Tariff & Market Access Guide

This skill guides customs duty determination, preferential origin verification, and non-tariff barrier compliance.

## 1. Customs Duty Precedence Hierarchy
When evaluating customs tariffs on a proposed shipment:
1. **Statutory Column 2 / Punitive Duties:** Applies to non-WTO/sanctioned origins or trade remedies (e.g., US Section 301 China tariffs).
2. **Most-Favoured-Nation (MFN) Applied Rate:** Standard default duty rate for all WTO members without a preferential agreement.
3. **Autonomous Suspensions / Quotas:** Temporary national duty suspensions for scarce raw materials.
4. **Generalised Scheme of Preferences (GSP):** Unilateral non-reciprocal concessions for developing/least developed nations (verify country graduation).
5. **Preferential Free Trade Agreement (FTA / EPA / CEPA):** Bilateral or regional zero/reduced duty rate, conditioned on strict proof of origin.

## 2. Rules of Origin (RoO) Assessment
Preferential tariff rates can ONLY be claimed if goods satisfy Product-Specific Rules (PSR):
- **Wholly Obtained (WO):** Goods harvested, grown, or born in the exporting territory.
- **Change in Tariff Heading (CTH / CTSH):** Non-originating materials must undergo substantial transformation into a different 4-digit heading (CTH) or 6-digit subheading (CTSH).
- **Regional Value Content (RVC):**
  $$\text{RVC} = \frac{\text{FOB Value} - \text{Value of Non-Originating Materials (VNM)}}{\text{FOB Value}} \times 100\%$$
- **Proof of Origin:** Determine mandatory documentary mechanism (e.g., Certificate of Origin Form A, EUR.1, Registered Exporter REX self-certification).

## 3. Sanitary & Phytosanitary (SPS) and Technical Barriers to Trade (TBT)
- **Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs):** Verify pesticide, mycotoxin, and heavy metal thresholds in destination jurisdictions.
- **Mandatory Inspections:** Determine origin-side export clearance certificates (e.g., FSSAI, APEDA, EIA in India).
- **Labeling & Conformity Directives:** Verify CE marking, nutritional facts language, country-of-origin marking, and organic certification recognition.
